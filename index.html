About Me

I am Akmalkhodja Zaynidinov — a pharmaceutical scientist and regulatory specialist with a mission to elevate the quality standards of medicines in Uzbekistan and Central Asia to the level of global best practices.

Since 2017, I have been at the forefront of developing the State Pharmacopoeia of the Republic of Uzbekistan — contributing to the drafting, editing, and international harmonization of over 40 monographs for active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), dosage forms, and general analytical methods.

As Chief Specialist at the Center for Pharmaceutical Product Safety, I combine deep analytical expertise with a passion for global collaboration — from WHO sessions in Delhi and Geneva to EDQM events in Strasbourg.

My goal is simple: ensure that every medicine reaching a patient meets the highest standards of quality, safety, and efficacy.
